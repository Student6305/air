# Five Key Insights for Hosts and Investors

**1. Premium-Priced Neighbourhoods**

The Top 5 neighbourhoods(Fairmount Park, Industrial District, Alki, Pike Place Market, Portage Bay) have the highest median listing prices in the dataset. These areas may offer opportunities for hosts targeting higher nightly rates, but high prices alone do not guarantee strong demand or profitability.

**2. More Affordable Neighbourhoods**

The Bottom 5 neighbourhoods(Victory Heights, Rainier Beach, Roxhill, Olympic Hills, Holly Park) have the lowest median listing prices. They may appeal to budget-conscious guests and investors seeking lower-priced opportunities, although occupancy and operating costs should also be considered.

**3. Potential Investment Targets**

Neighbourhoods with both above-median listing prices and above-median occupancy rates are potential investment targets. They combine relatively strong pricing with demand and should be investigated further before making investment decisions.

**4. Price and Occupancy Should Be Evaluated Together**

A high listing price does not necessarily mean a property will be booked frequently. Hosts should consider both pricing and occupancy when setting nightly rates, rather than focusing on price alone.

**5. Profitability Requires Further Analysis**

Median price and occupancy help identify promising locations, but they do not show net profit. Investors should also evaluate property purchase costs, cleaning and maintenance expenses, seasonal demand, and applicable local regulations before choosing a neighbourhood.
