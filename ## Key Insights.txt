## Key Insights

- **Booking Performance:** Booking data helps identify customer booking patterns and understand the overall booking rate.
- **Revenue Potential:** Analyzing prices and booking status helps estimate revenue generated by confirmed bookings and identify potential business opportunities.
- **Price Analysis:** Grouping prices into different bands makes it easier to compare booking performance across low-, medium-, and high-priced options.
- **Data-Driven Decisions:** Visualizations and conditional formatting make important trends and differences easier to identify.
- **Business Recommendations:** Businesses can use these insights to improve pricing strategies, increase bookings, and make more informed decisions.
